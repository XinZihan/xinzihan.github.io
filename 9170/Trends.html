Finding Community Discussions and Emerging Topics

This page has two parts. The first introduces public discussion spaces related to marginalized communities. The second presents a sample trend identified through visible online discussions.

Remember that online discussions do not represent every member of a community. When searching for information, we should use online discussion resources as starting points, we should not treat them as complete evidence of community opinion.

Available Community Discussion Places

We can visit public forums to learn how people describe their own questions and experiences. These spaces can help you identify current language, community resources, and concerns that may be difficult to locate through formal sources.

Community area Discussion place What you may find
Indigenous communities r/IndigenousCanada Discussions about Indigenous culture, politics, community issues, history, and current events.
Overseas Chinese communities Wenxuecity Forums 文学城 Chinese-language discussions about immigration, family life, education, health, employment, and life in Canada.
Newcomers to Canada Newcomers to Canada — Facebook Questions about settling in Canada, services, employment, housing, and daily life.
LGBTQ+ communities in Toronto Queer Toronto — Facebook Local LGBTQ+ events, community news, resources, projects, and personal discussions.
Transgender communities r/asktransgender Questions and peer discussions about transgender identity, healthcare, transition, family, and daily life.
Older adults AARP Online Community Conversations about caregiving, health, technology, retirement, relationships, and aging.
Older adult perspectives r/AskOldPeople Questions answered by older adults about social change, relationships, work, and life experience.
Disability communities r/disability Discussions about accessibility, benefits, healthcare, identity, employment, and independent living.
Before you participate We should always read the rules of each community, respect the community culture. And friendly remind, some forums may restrict promotional posts, requests for interviews, or direct messages to community members.

A highly active forum is not automatically more representative or more reliable than a smaller community space.
